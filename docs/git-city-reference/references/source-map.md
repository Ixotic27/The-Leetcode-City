## Town grid and editable world

- [src/lib/league-city/grid.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/grid.ts): `LOT = 48`, starting half-width 6, maximum 20; inclusive lots x = [-h,h], z = [-2h+1,0]. World bounds use lot edges; terrain center derives from those bounds. Rotation is clockwise from north; Three.js yaw negates that angle. Growth threshold is 70 percent occupancy.
- [src/lib/league-city/types.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/types.ts): `CityObject` stores lot coordinates for surfaces/buildings and optional world-space px/pz for props. `CityOp` describes placement/move/rotate/remove/expand operations. Identity includes sky, logo and hill-sign side.
- [src/lib/league-city/starter.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/starter.ts): crew = main/cross streets with entrance plazas; race = loop, ramps, boost pads, bumps and tire walls; hq = downtown blocks; park = plazas/trees; blank = entrance/portal. Member buildings face roads, larger buildings enter first, scenery fills afterward. Entrance lots and portal are locked; approach roads extend outside the editable grid.
- [src/lib/league-city/roads.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/roads.ts): four-neighbor bitmask selects single/end/straight/corner/tee/cross and rotation. [placement.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/placement.ts) owns road-facing placement.
- [src/components/league/LeagueScene.tsx](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/components/league/LeagueScene.tsx): shared Canvas, data-derived ground/camera framing, view/edit/preview/drive modes, dynamic drive bundle, demand rendering while editing, instanced decorations, roads, trees, identity and building layer. View/edit towns use rectangular terrain with a fogged void; do not mistake that renderer for the geographic island renderer.

## Actual island construction

- [src/lib/towns/brasil-geo.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/towns/brasil-geo.ts): projected outline/state rings from simplified IBGE geography, coast-distance queries, map bounds including Fernando de Noronha, scale-aware `geoAt`, cached `landGrid` with coastal margin. Real geography is a specific Brasil feature, not necessary for every procedural island.
- [src/lib/towns/brasil-map.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/towns/brasil-map.ts): `BrasilMap` owns resident placement, occupied blocks, road continuity, mainland land checks, separation and increasing scale. Island hub is pushed out to sea; island blocks require coast clearance. `layout()` returns cities, island and highways. Island radius derives from occupied blocks with a minimum radius.
- [src/lib/towns/brasil-world.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/towns/brasil-world.ts): converts layout into reusable town objects/buildings, street/country trees, lamps/fountains, highways and palms around island. Seeded scenery; drift site is excluded from woods.
- [src/components/towns/brasil/BrasilScene.tsx](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/components/towns/brasil/BrasilScene.tsx), `Ground`, around lines 103-239: mainland uses `ShapeGeometry`. Resident island centers on its hub; base radius is `(island.radius + 1.5) * LOT`; 48 angular points vary radius with two sine terms. The drift island uses 64 rounded-square points fitted to `DRIFT_SITE` plus margin. A sand ribbon follows each shore. Sea is below sand, sand below land, preventing coplanar overlap. Resources are memoized and disposed.
- The same scene uses shared water materials, merged static terrain by material, instanced paving/plazas, nearby labels, bounded tree physics and low/high DPR. It reuses `DriveWorld` for ordinary driving and the drift world when entering the track.
- [src/components/drift/HarborGround.tsx](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/components/drift/HarborGround.tsx): simpler island encloses track bounds with a circular radius plus 70 meters, sand ring and grass disk over a sea plane. Physics-to-render unit conversion happens explicitly.
- [src/lib/drift/spots/brasil.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/drift/spots/brasil.ts) and [src/lib/towns/brasil-drift.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/towns/brasil-drift.ts): drift circuit and geographic site definitions. The course is built from straights/arcs and positioned at the shared site; it is driveable, not a decorative racing cabinet.

## Car simulation and controls

- [src/lib/league-city/drive/tuning.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/drive/tuning.ts): 1 city unit = 0.4 meters. Central chassis, wheel, engine, steering, road/plaza/grass/wet/asphalt surface, drift, boost and recovery tuning.
- [src/lib/league-city/drive/vehicle.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/drive/vehicle.ts): pure per-step Rapier DynamicRayCastVehicleController logic; four raycast wheels, rear drive, surface grip, held drift, banked race mini-turbo, flip recovery. - [src/components/league/drive/useDriveInput.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/components/league/drive/useDriveInput.ts): keyboard/gamepad/touch merge; typing guard; blur cleanup; short taps survive between frames; held levels and rising-edge actions are distinct.
- [src/lib/league-city/drive/net.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/drive/net.ts): room per town, compact snapshots about every 66 ms, 110 ms driver interpolation, spectators batched at 200 ms with 320 ms delay, caps and typed protocol. These are network design choices, not guaranteed latency or authority over client movement.
- [src/lib/league-city/drive/rally.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/drive/rally.ts): in-town route/checkpoint racing; [rally.test.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/drive/rally.test.ts) is useful behavioral reference. Crown, heist and battle are separate modules, not required dependencies of ordinary driving.

## Dedicated track racing

- [src/lib/league-city/race/track.ts](https://github.com/srizzon/git-city/blob/98cab64cf834eb1d7e6bec2302a468c0138ab841/src/lib/league-city/race/track.ts): `TrackSpec` supports closed loops/open courses, centerline points, width/runoff/walls/checkpoints/grid; samples Catmull-Rom at about 2 meters; stores distance-along-track and spatial index. ## Suggested local adoption order (local design decisions)

1. Fix existing coordinate, picking, bounds and camera ownership faults; preserve working LeetCode additions.
2. Profile and bound repeated effects, scenery, labels, CPU raycasting and frame-loop allocations; unify quality rather than stacking toggles.
3. Improve island silhouette/shore spacing from actual occupied extents; keep roads, water, spawn and minimap in one coordinate frame.
4. Add local driving with an isolated shared input/simulation/controller boundary and coherent collisions. Introduce Rapier only if its physics is being used; keep it out of ordinary browse bundles.
5. Add a playable checkpoint time trial with countdown/reset/finish; reuse the shared track for visuals, collision and minimap, then add bounded ghosts.
6. Add persistent competitive records and multiplayer only with the corresponding service/data/validation pieces. League memberships, Brasil geography, elections, heists, monetization and destructive world mechanics are optional separate products.

All recommendations above are adaptations for the local project, not claims that its present code already implements them. Source inspection establishes architecture, not local FPS or runtime correctness.
